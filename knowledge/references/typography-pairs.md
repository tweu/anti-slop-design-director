# Typography pairings

These are starting points, grouped by the world they evoke. Unless noted, every family is on Google Fonts (OFL) and can be
self-hosted. Avoid defaulting to the same pairing across projects. If you used one recently, choose another
that fits equally well.

## Choosing rules
- The display face carries the voice and the text face carries the reading. If they compete, one is wrong.
- Check the needed weights and scripts (Cyrillic, Greek, Vietnamese) before committing.
- Pair across contrast (serif with sans, wide with narrow), or use one superfamily. Two similar sans faces look like a mistake.
If installed, UI/UX Pro Max `--domain typography` has 74 more pairings and `--domain google-fonts` covers about 1,900 families.
